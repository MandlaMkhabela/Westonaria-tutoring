Frequently asked questions

How do I get my videos and notes after paying?

You're redirected straight back to a delivery page with your YouTube playlist link and your Google Drive folder (notes, memos, and past papers). Save that page — you can also request the links again via WhatsApp with your proof of payment.

What payment methods are accepted?

Card, Instant EFT, and SnapScan/Zapper — all processed securely through Yoco. We never see or store your card details.

Are refunds available?

Because these are digital products delivered instantly, all sales are final once the link is unlocked. If a link doesn't work, message us on WhatsApp and we'll fix it right away.

Which curriculum are these based on?

CAPS-aligned content covering the Grade 11 and Grade 12 (Matric) syllabus set by the Department of Basic Education. This is an independent resource and isn't affiliated with the DBE.

Not sure which pack you need?
